# Standardizing the Galactic Year
The [Curators](../../../../Factions/Curators/curators.md) set the galactic standard year on the year it took their homeworld to orbit their star because who was gonna argue against it anyways. This was 289.35 32 Hour Days.

At some point, the Curators decided to demolish their homeworld and rebuild it into megastructure inside VEIL -2 for their main settlement. This removed the ability to measure the galactic standard year. Once the Curators disappeared, there was no one left to enforce this.

## Restandardizing the Galactic Year
Once the galaxy had mostly discovered each other and established relationships, the [Cyroni](../../../../Factions/Cyronia/cyroni.md) who were fed up with the constant mix-ups with time, proposed a galactic standard year and proposed it be set to the Year of the Ark's orbit around the parent star (which just so happened to be exactly the Curator's galactic standard). No one agreed to this because no one saw why they should adopt the year of another species.

The [Niox](../../../../Factions/Niox/niox.md) proposed the galactic year be set to their year because they were the chosen successors of the Curators. No one agreed to this. After months of the Cyroni and Niox arguing about what the year should be set to, the Cyroni discovered the original galactic year which was stored in the Citadel on the [Ark](../../../../Locations/ark.md). After much more arguing about what the year should be, a Cyroni diplomat argued to the Niox: "If you're really the chosen heirs of the Curators, you'll adopt their galactic year".

The Niox, unable to argue against the point, but not wanting to yield to these obvious Curator posers, proposed the Callarans pick the year since they were neutral. Ironically, the Callarans, being lazy, opted to just go with the old galactic year since it was the easiest solution. Then the Callarans went on Holiday for the rest of the year. And thus, the galactic standard year was set to what it always had been.

All governments adopted the galactic year with the exception of the [Zol'Nos](../../../../Factions/ZolNos/zolnos.md) who didn't care much for the politics of the galaxy.
