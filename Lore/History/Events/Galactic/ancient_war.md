# The War of the Ancients
Legend has it long before the current races ever entered the space age, there was a war between two races on the galactic scale. One of them was the [Curators](../../../../Factions/Curators/curators.md), the other being codenamed as the Unkindred. The two had opposing ideologies: the Curators believed in preservation and the right for lesser species to exist. The Unkindred, however believed races who fell behind or those who could not fend for themselves, should not exist in the galaxy and therefore committed to their annihilation. The Curators could not abide by the Unkindred’s philosophy and declared they would send them back to the stone age. The Unkindred met the challenge, determined to prove they were the superior race.

The war lasted 5 decades, Nicol-Dyson Beams and other super-weapons were used in the war. Many planets were scorched, many star systems were outright obliterated. Eventually, the Curators discovered it was possible to open a permanent aperture into the [VEIL](../../../Tech/VEIL/veil.md) using black-holes. They decided to lay a trap, they would lure the primary force of the Unkindred to the aperture, which the Unkindred believed would lead straight to the Curators’ homeworld. They took the bait, as the massive fleet passed through, the Curators detonated the Aperture, causing all ships who travelled through to be instantly annihilated and the first [Dead Zone](../../../Tech/VEIL/dead_zones.md) was created. With the bulk of the Unkindred fleet destroyed, the Curators wiped through their territory all the way back to the Unkindred’s homeworld. 

When all was said and done, the Curators decided the Unkindred’s philosophy was not compatible with the future of the galaxy and banished the remaining Unkindred to a satellite-galaxy which was accessible via the old VEIL network. Once the relocation was complete, the lane towards the satellite-galaxy was mined with physics-mines and sealed off by creating a deliberate dead-zone, blocking the Unkindred from ever returning.
