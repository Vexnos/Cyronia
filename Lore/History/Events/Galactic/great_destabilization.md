# The Great Destabilization
## The Forge
The [Curators](../../../../Factions/Curators/curators.md) constructed the Forge in the system of [Zol'Nozh](../../../../Locations/zolnozh.md). However, they had become complacent, rushing the development of the Forge. They didn't thoroughly study the Quasar of Zol'Quos, they didn't know that Black Hole had a personality, it had a temper and it was ready to strike back. The first test runs yielded profitable results. Before the final portal frame was constructed (the portal needed to be large to fabricate large ships and megastructures), they used a smaller test frame than the intended final size. This test frame ended up being repurposed as the Ark, as was intended. This is why the Ark has a ring section, the arms were added later.

## Chaos in the Zol'Nozh System
Once the test runs were complete, the Curators constructed the final frame. However, in their haste, Zol'Quos awoke and struck back. A large blast of energy was ejected from the Quasar, while Curator hulls and shields were incredibly robust, they were no match for the might of the Raging Quasar. All ships and facilities were completely destroyed, save the Ark which had already moved to its intended destination a few decades prior. Zol'Nozh, a lush planet was scarred by this blast, however, the planet was not destroyed due to it being a lot further away than the Forge.

A planetary shield on Zol'Nozh (which was made to keep them in as punishment) made by the Curators saved the [Zol'Nos](../../../../Factions/ZolNos/zolnos.md) from the blast and ensuing radiation. However, the shield was rendered permanently inoperable afterwards as it was not designed to take the force of a Quasar. The Zol'Nos regarded the Curators as responsible for the scarring of their world. Thus, an already incredible enraged species vowed revenge and the subjugation or annihilation of all species the Curators protected. This doctrine was passed down for thousands of generations.

## The VEIL Upset
Because Black Holes have links with [VEIL](../../../Tech/VEIL/veil.md), Zol'Quos also caused havoc in VEIL. Like a large underwater earthquake, Zol'Quos upset the whole dimension, causing it to twist and turn. Any ship travelling through was crushed. The Curators' settlements in VEIL -2 were safer than what was happening in VEIL1 and deeper (in fact, deeper levels were affected much more). The Curators had ordered all members of their species into the pocket dimensions while the Forge was being tested as a safety precaution. Unfortunately, this was a poor decision as the cataclysm reorganized VEIL completely, also destroying all other entrances to pocket dimensions since black holes are required to enter them. This is what created all the dead zones around the galaxy; open tears into VEIL. This meant the Curators were forever locked away, never to return.
