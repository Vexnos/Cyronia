# VEIL Dwellers
Legend has it that there is a sapient species which originates from the [VEIL](./veil.md). They have never been seen in person, and are incredibly illusive and isolationist. [Cyronian](../../../Factions/Cyronia/cyroni.md) Artists have drafted concepts to depict their appearance, but none have ever been verified. Some Cyroni think they are fantasy. 

The [Niox](../../../Factions/Niox/niox.md) believe they are somehow the remnants of the [Curators](../../../Factions/Curators/curators.md) who discovered how to exist in VEIL and do not want to return to the galaxy. As such, the Niox are incredibly desperate to establish contact with them.
