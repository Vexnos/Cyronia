# VEIL Dwellers
Legend has it that there is a sapient species which originates from the [VEIL](./veil.md). They have never been seen in person, and are incredibly illusive and isolationist. Cyronian Artists have drafted concepts to depict their appearance, but none have ever been verified. Some Cyroni think they are fantasy. 

The Niox believe they are somehow the remnants of the Curators who discovered how to exist in VEIL and do not want to return to the galaxy. As such, the Niox are incredibly desperate to establish contact with them.
