# The VEIL - Vectored Extradimensional Interlaced Loci
The VEIL-Space is another dimension with its own laws of physics. It’s a blue, murky, foggy dimension. Legend has it that creatures inhabit the dimension, some even claim those of the leviathan scale exist. VEIL is the galaxy-wide network of fixed anchor points with lanes linking to each point. Ships use these points to enter and exit VEIL-Space for FTL travel, while using these fixed points is not strictly mandatory, doing otherwise requires lengthy calculations. Even with quantum-computers, this calculation can take from hours to days to complete.

VEIL-capable ships use a fairly rudimentary device known as an ‘Extradimensional Boundary Twister’ (or EBT) to open an ‘enter’ or ‘exit’ aperture to transition between VEIL-space and realspace and vice-versa. The constraint to the duration of your stay in VEIL-space is how long you can maintain a physics field around your ship, preserving your laws of physics. The galactic-standard is to use a large device known as a ‘Phase-Shifter’ which generates a slight ‘dimensional-pocket’ field around your ship on the interdimensional boundary between VEIL-space and realspace, allowing realspace physics to exist in that field. Phase-Shifters require massive amounts of power to run continuously and therefore require strong, efficient reactors. The minimum class of a VEIL-capable ship is a corvette. Fighters and Shuttles are not VEIL-capable as they are too small to house the necessary parts. In theory, ships with larger and better reactors are faster in VEIL-space, but in practise, it also depends on other design considerations for your ship. You cannot open an Aperture in atmosphere as you have to combat atmospheric pressure which have a serious risk of permanent catastrophic ship disassembly.

VEIL exists in multiple levels. The higher the level, the faster you travel across the galaxy. The Curators only reached VEIL-5, but believed there are up to 9 VEIL levels. The current galaxy is at VEIL-1, they do not know about the higher levels.
