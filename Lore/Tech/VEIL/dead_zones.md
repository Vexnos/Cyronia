# Dead Zones
Dead Zones are galactic wounds formed from open tears into the [VEIL](./veil.md), they are very deadly and unpredictable areas in space. They used to be permanent-apertures around black holes into [Curator](../../../Factions/Curators/curators.md) pocket dimensions. When the great destabilization happened, these portals were destroyed, causing these tears. If any ship attempts travel in these areas in realspace, destruction is highly likely, thus 99.9% of ships avoid this area. The equivalent location in the VEIL is unknown as Curator settlements were never located after the great destabilization.
