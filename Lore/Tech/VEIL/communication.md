# Galactic Communication
The Galactic Communication Relay Network (GCRN) was established by the Curators via a network of megastructural-sized relays spread across the galaxy. These relays had the ability to peer into [VEIL](./veil.md) to send signals across the galaxy. Each relay in realspace would have a counterpart relay in its equivalent position in VEIL. Every VEIL relay is connected via long, shielded cables which carry signals to other relays since carrier waves do not work the same as in realspace. This system requires that you enter the approximate location of your intended receiver alongside your message. Real time communication is almost possible, but there are slight delays (imagine a high ping discord call) despite the efficiency of the curator network.

If you wanted to send a signal, it would first travel to the realspace relay where it would be redirected to the relay in VEIL. The signal would be sent through the cables to its target relay and that relay would send the signal to its realspace counterpart. 

The GCRN is how current spacefarers initially explored and charted the VEIL since curator relays were already in their realspace counterpart’s position. While using the relays was fairly trivial as the Curators allowed other species to use their network, not a single race has discovered how the relays actually work, or how to control one. 

Since the cables need to exist in VEIL permanently, they are incredibly durable (as is most Curator tech) and heavily shielded (yes the Curators have shields and I made it a high tier technology because it’s basically space magic anyways). As far as the current spacefarers, they might as well be indestructible.
