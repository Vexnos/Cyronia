# The Unkindred
The name 'Unkindred' is a codename assigned to the species by the current races. The Curator language has not been deciphered yet so no one knows the actual name of the species.

## Info
The Unkindred were an ancient race on par with the [Curators](../Curators/curators.md). They were the Antithesis to their beliefs of preservation, virtue, and peace. They believed lesser species were to be subjugated or to be exterminated. Only the most powerful had the right to dictate the galaxy.

## Exiles
The Unkindred eventually lost the [Ancient War](../../Lore/History/Events/Galactic/ancient_war.md) against the Curators. The Curators deemed it was not proper for such a violent race to remain in the galaxy. Thus, they banished the Unkindred to a satellite galaxy and mined the whole VEIL highway back, ensuring the Unkindred could never return. Many Curator installations have anti-unkindred measures if they should ever return.
