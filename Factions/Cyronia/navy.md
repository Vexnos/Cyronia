## Lore
While the Ziktar heavy cruiser proved to be incredibly effective in its deployment against the Zol'Nos, there were calls amongst the Caretakers for a ship that could finally outrange their nemesis' main energy cannon, and focus more on sheer ship destruction at the expense of some operational independence. This came in the form of the Vulkar Heavy Destroyer, quickly dominating the battlespace. This ship and the Ziktar Cruiser were influential in the Cyroni liberating Terizin.
