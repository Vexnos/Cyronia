## Registration Convention
The Niox navy works on a pattern class system, noting what purpose the ship fills in the navy. This allows Niox ships to be very versatile and modular, making their navy incredibly adaptable to their foe.

Example: ABC-####

| Letter | Purpose | Use |
| ------ | ------- | --- |
| A | Assault | Can operate independently or lead planetary invasions. |
| B | Bulwark | Does not operate independently, used for planetary defense. |
| C | Combat | Outfitted for ship-to-ship combat. |
| E | Exploration | Can operate independently and houses equipment to chart and explore regions. |
| O | Optimized | Ships operating with Curator technology. (Rare)
| P | Patrol | Ships designated for border patrols. |
| S | Scourging | Can glass planets. |
| V | VEIL | Capable of discharging torpedoes from VEIL. |
