## Info
The Callarans are a unique oddity among the galaxy, being what amounts to human-sized gelatinous orange blobs without any kind of face. Their government is unique in how it runs like a corporation rather than a standard government. Their capital, Callara is a large-mobile ecumenopolis featuring many attractions and even giving the [Niox](../Niox/niox.md)’s [Nexus](../../Locations/nexus.md) a run for its money. They allow all races to browse and trade on Callara, unless of course they disturb the peace. 

The Callaran Trade Union is not your cliche mustache-twirling evil corporation, their employees have rights, benefits, annual paid vacation and sick-leave, and even subsidised poetry-classes. They truly are the pinnacle of galactic commerce. However, it’s rare to actually see a Callaran in person as their paid holidays account for 243 days, or exactly one Callaran year and everyone is usually on holiday and they just get robots to do their jobs instead. This is the societal norm and no one cares about it because the robots do a better job anyways.
